Browser Automation & Assistance

Alfie

Available Now

A more natural way to work with your browser. Turn plain-language instructions into clear, step-by-step actions with complete local control.

Key Capabilities

  • 100% On-Device Execution: Planning runs locally inside your browser process with zero prompt telemetry.
  • Keyboard-First Navigation: Quick page mapping with visible numbered shortcut chips (Alt+Shift+M).
  • Ask First Safety Gate: Sensitive actions pause for affirmative user approval before executing.
  • On-Device Voice Input: Speak instructions directly with local speech recognition (Alt+Shift+S).
