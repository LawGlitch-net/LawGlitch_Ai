Product Experience

Designed for everyday browser tasks.

Alfie eliminates tedious clicking, forms, and repetitive navigation so you can focus on the work that matters.

Keyboard-First Control

Navigate and interact with page controls at the speed of thought. Press shortcuts to index interactive elements with visual numbered chips and execute without hunting for tiny buttons.

Type or Speak Naturally

Give plain-language instructions or use your voice. Alfie parses what you want to achieve on the current page and outlines a clear sequence of steps to get it done.

Step-By-Step Workflow

How Alfie works in your browser.

A transparent 3-step loop that keeps you in command of every click and keystroke.

Step 1

Tell Alfie what you want

Type a natural prompt or press Alt+Shift+S to speak your instruction directly into the floating assistant.

Step 2

Review the planned action

Alfie maps the page elements locally and presents the planned actions with highlighted target elements.

Step 3

Confirm and execute safely

Approve sensitive actions with one click or let routine steps proceed with visible progress receipts.

Local-First Architecture

Built for local execution. Not cloud telemetry.

The base Alfie extension is engineered to run locally in your browser. Your instructions, prompts, and page context are evaluated on your own machine.

No Third-Party AI Routing for Base Product

Unlike cloud-based assistant extensions that stream complete web pages and session cookies to external servers, Alfie’s base product processes actions on-device.

Zero Marketing Tracking

Neither this website nor the base extension use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or behavioral analytics cookies.

Clear Distinction for Future Cloud Features

Any optional cloud-backed AI features or Pro capabilities introduced in the future will be explicitly disclosed before launch with opt-in controls.
